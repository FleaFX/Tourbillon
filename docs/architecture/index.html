Tourbillon

Architecture

This book explains how Tourbillon works and why, in plain language, with the choices I made along the way and the measurements behind them. The chapters build on each other, so read them in order. The book describes the current design; when the design changes, the book changes first.

Chapters

  1. What is TourbillonThe problem it solves, what it takes from Raft and what it adds, and what it is not.
  2. The log on one machineSegments, fsync and group commit, recovery after a crash, and a full disk.
  3. State and snapshotsHow the application gets the committed entries, keeps its own state, and lets go of the start of the log.
  4. Several machinesThe core of Raft: groups, the leader and the majority, elections, and the three outcomes of a proposal.
  5. Changing the replicasMembership changes one replica at a time, learners, leadership transfer, and PreVote.
  6. Hosting and APIMany groups in one process, the network through the host, the lent feed, settings, measurements, and the test package.
